## Featured Project

### [DocuGuardian](https://github.com/ankit-21310/DocuGuardian) — AI Document Protection Agent
`OpenAI Build Week · team of 5`
[![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Code-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/ankit-21310/DocuGuardian)
[![Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Demo-FF0000?style=flat-square&logo=youtube&logoColor=white)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q4ZNR_g1abs)
[![Devpost](https://img.shields.io/badge/Devpost-003E54?style=flat-square&logo=devpost&logoColor=white)](https://devpost.com/software/docuguardian)

An end-to-end document intelligence platform that goes past summarization — it surfaces hidden risks, penalties, and deadlines in loan agreements, insurance policies, and contracts *before* costly decisions get made.

- **OCR ingestion → classification → structured extraction** pipeline for PDFs and scanned documents, pulling parties, dates, monetary values, interest rates, obligations, and clauses.
- **Cross-document reasoning** layer that understands relationships between multiple uploaded documents instead of treating each in isolation.
- Dedicated models for **risk scoring, clause severity detection, and deadline extraction**, with every finding grounded in an exact document reference to control hallucination.
- Multilingual translation and AI voice summaries to make legal and financial text accessible to non-English readers.

`Python` `FastAPI` `RAG` `Vector Search` `OCR` `PostgreSQL` `Redis` `Docker` `Next.js` `TypeScript`
